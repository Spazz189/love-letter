Across the distance

Even with all the distance between us, I catch myself thinking about you at random moments during the day, and every single time, I’m overwhelmed by how deeply I love you. It’s hard to put into words because what I feel for you keeps growing, even across the miles, and even when I think my heart is already completely full, somehow I wake up loving you even more than I did yesterday.

I want you to know how truly thankful I am to have you in my life. Thank you Mommy for always listening to me, for making every call feel like home, and for being my safe space through a screen when the world gets noisy. You understand me in ways no one else ever has, and your voice alone can quiet my worst days.

I can’t wait for the day we finally get to close this distance and I get to hold you for real. But until then, please remember that you mean everything to me, Mommy. Out of everyone and everything in this life, you are the person I love the absolute most.

Our tomorrow

Furthermore, each and every single day, you’re always the first thing on my mind when I wake up and the last thought that warms my nights. I know distance might keep us from touching, but it can’t stop you from filling up every single moment of my day. You mean the absolute world to me, and living this life with you, even through calls and screens, you're my favorite thing.

Often I catch myself daydreaming about our future all the time, and it makes me so excited for what’s ahead. I can’t wait for the day we get to travel the world together, see new places, and build a lifetime of memories side by side. I look forward to all the silly, funny moments we’ll share—the effortless laughter, the random inside jokes, and all the goofy everyday things we haven't been able to do in person yet.

Lastly and most of all, I dream of the family we’re going to build. I want to spend the rest of my life fulfilling every single plan and dream we've talked about, and there is no one else I’d ever want to start a family with or become a dad with except you. You are my future, Mommy, and I'm counting down every second until it all comes true.

Always you

No matter what life throws our way, I need you to know one thing above all else is that I will always be here for you. Through every high, every low, every quiet victory, and every hard day, I will stand right by your side. You will never have to face this world alone because you will always, without a doubt, have me forever.

The love I have for you isn't something that fades with time or distance. It’s written into who I am. Whatever circumstances come our way, whatever challenges life brings, I promise that I will choose you, every single day, over and over again, without hesitation.

From the very second I open my eyes in the morning to the moment I close them at night, and even in every dream in between, it will always be you. You are my home, my choice, and the love of my life, today and for all the tomorrows to come.

For you, always. ♡
